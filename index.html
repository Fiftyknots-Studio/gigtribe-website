For teams

Lead a project, or support one.

Every tribe can take either role, project by project.

Leading Tribe

Your tribe owns the project. You send the proposal, run delivery for the client and bring in support tribes when the brief needs skills you don't have in-house.

Support Tribe

Your tribe joins another tribe's project to fill a skills gap or put spare capacity to work, delivering its part alongside the leading tribe.
